# Design Tokens — Mendoza Lawn & Tree Service

Forked from Template Zero. Full re-run of the token brainstorm — distinct from Jurassic, Master Bin, and Opulence in this batch.

## 1. Identity

Mendoza Lawn & Tree Service, Denton, TX. Real address (1114 Hopkins Dr) and hours (Mon–Fri 9–6) found in research, plus real Google reviews via Birdeye. Reviewers specifically praise responsiveness and cleanup/attention-to-detail, and one names tree removal (with stump grinding) as the standout job. A visitor should feel, in three seconds, that this is a capable, fast-responding local crew that does both the big dramatic job (a tree coming down) and the quiet weekly one (mowing) equally well.

## 2. Palette concept

The obvious literal read is plain "grass green." Grounded it more specifically instead: the actual color story of a live-oak-canopy Texas yard — deep canopy shadow, sunlit clearing, cut-grass green, and bark brown as an equal second accent (not a rare pop) since tree work and lawn work are genuinely two co-equal service lines here, not one specialty with an add-on.

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Canopy** | `#16211A` | Base dark surface — tree-shadow green-black |
| **Clearing** | `#F2EDDD` | Base light surface — sunlit clearing cream |
| **Ink** | `#14201A` | Body text on light surfaces |
| **Cut Grass** | `#5B7A3A` | Primary accent — CTAs, the lawn-care half of the business |
| **Heartwood Bark** | `#8A5A36` | Equal secondary accent — the tree-service half; used as often as Cut Grass, not sparingly |
| **Moss** | `#7A8770` | Muted support — borders, secondary text |

## 3. Type pairing

- **Display:** Bitter (600/700) — a sturdy, grounded slab serif with a slightly rustic, trustworthy feel.
- **Body:** Nunito Sans — warm, rounded, friendly, legible.

**Why this pairing:** this is an established, trusted local crew (real reviews, real hours), not a brand-new startup (Jurassic) or a refined boutique (Opulence) or a minimal utility (Master Bin). Bitter reads as sturdy and outdoorsy without the industrial edge of Jurassic's condensed face or the luxury of Opulence's italic serif.

## 4. Signature element: none (dropped, per brief's own option)

Per the brief: "modify or remove... depending on what fits each identity; don't just leave it as-is." This build's structural device — **a fixed, full-bleed canopy photo staying in place behind the whole page, with alternating transparent and solid panels scrolling over it** — already carries the cross-section continuity a rail element would provide. Adding a left-edge rail on top of a parallax background would compete visually with the panels rather than support them, so it was dropped entirely rather than forced in. This is this batch's explicitly no-rail build.

## 5. Structural choices

- **Layout style assigned:** one dominant fixed full-bleed image (the oak-canopy tree crew photo) with alternating transparent/solid panels scrolling over it.
- **Lead section:** Tree Service, directly under the hero — of the two service lines, tree removal is the one reviewers name specifically and by job detail ("two trees taken down with stumps removed"), so it leads per the brief's rule about promoting the most specifically-praised specialty.
- **Lawn care:** kept as the clear second line, not buried — reviews praise it too ("lawn and trees always look fantastic"), so it gets its own full section, just after tree service rather than before it.
- **Reviews:** two real, specific quotes used with real first-name-plus-initial attribution (Nikko M., Glenda E.) matching how the actual public reviews were signed — not invented.
- **About:** kept moderate, not expanded into a personal founder story — no specific owner name was confirmed in research (a customer mentions "Ivan" doing work, but nothing confirms he's the owner rather than a crew member), so the copy describes "a crew," not a named individual, to avoid asserting something unverified.

## 6. Images

All real stock (Unsplash) — a matched set from what appears to be one photoshoot of a live-oak tree crew (visually consistent with a Texas canopy), plus a real manicured-lawn shot. No AI-generated imagery.

**Re-checked 2026-07-30:** actively searched for Mendoza's own real photos (Facebook page — blocked without login, Birdeye — profile is unclaimed with only a generic stock cover image and no photo gallery). Genuinely nothing usable found this time, unlike the Opulence build in this same batch where real photos existed and weren't checked hard enough initially.

## 7. Flag for Owen

Two things worth checking before outreach:
1. **Rating discrepancy:** the brief states 5.0★/13 reviews; independent research (Birdeye/Google) found **4.7★ across 13 reviews**, with one negative review from ~2 years ago about staff conduct (most recent reviews, within the past year, are all strongly positive). Worth confirming the current live rating before it's quoted anywhere in outreach.
2. **No phone number found** in public research — only the address and hours. The demo site currently has no phone number displayed, same gap as Master Bin.
